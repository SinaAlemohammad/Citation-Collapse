## Metric definitions

- **overlap** — mean over 120 records of `|5-grams(paraphrase) ∩ 5-grams(original)| / |5-grams(original)|`. Low = surface form destroyed.
- **coreness_r** — Pearson correlation of the coreness index (core-method minus applied-domain term density per 100 words) before vs after paraphrasing. High = meaning preserved.
- **len_change** — mean relative change in abstract word count.
- **diag** — fraction of records whose paraphrase is most word-similar to its **own** original among all 120. <100% = content drifted into a different paper.
